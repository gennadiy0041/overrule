## Post

In the last 24 hours I let AI write most of my code. The part that was mine was deciding when it was wrong.

That is the product I built, solo, at Business Cup 2.0 by @Zimran and @AIESEC in Kazakhstan.

**Overrule** — a 5-minute daily drill for the skill people are now paid for: reviewing AI's work.

→ You get a document from your own job, "written by AI".
→ It hides 3–4 mistakes — and a few lines that only look wrong.
→ You flag what's actually wrong. Code scores you against an answer key. Your Judgment Score moves.

Why this, why now: 58% of US workers rely on AI output without properly checking it, and 57% have already made
mistakes at work because of AI (KPMG, 2025). Courses teach you to do more with AI. Nobody trains the other half —
knowing when to overrule it.

Two things I'd love your help with:

https://github.com/gennadiy0041/overrule

What did AI get wrong for you this week?
